A few things to know.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Start with your pipe material, diameter range, wall thickness and required output. Application, raw material grade, plant space and utilities help define the line configuration. Add any special requirements to your inquiry brief.

Can the whole production line be configured?

Yes. The offering covers turnkey extrusion solutions, including extruders and downstream equipment such as calibration tanks, haul-offs, cutters, coilers and belling units. The final equipment scope depends on your pipe specification.

Can I review real machine footage and certifications?

Request actual testing videos, available running-line references and applicable certification documents for the model you are evaluating.

How do I send my inquiry?

Complete the form and download your inquiry brief. Email it to sales@hdextrusion.com or reach our team through the contact page.
