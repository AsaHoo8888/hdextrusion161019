Plastic Pipe Caterpillars Haul Off Machine

Pull extruded pipe through the line with caterpillar belts.

Inquire about this product Prepare a detailed project brief

Equipment overview

Caterpillar haul-offs from 2 to 10 belts

Depending on pad type, dual caterpillar haul-offs can be used for pipes or profiles.

Multiple caterpillar haul-offs are used for pipe take-off.

The lower caterpillar carriers are adjusted manually, the upper caterpillar carriers are suspended and are pneumatically adjusted.

Speed:- Wide range of speed available with AC vary drive / servo drive is mainly for accuracy & high range of speed.

Synchronization:- Effective synchronization is provided for Haul-off and extruders.

Independent drive for each caterpillar chain with A.C. motors and vectorial drive (elimination of transmission elements subject to wear - minimal maintenance and highest reliability); All the caterpillar chains are pulling, and consist of rubber blocks, which are especially shaped to obtain the largest surface in contact with the pipe;
