Equipment overview

Strong Plastic PE / PVC pulverizer machine

PVC/PE plastic pulverizer is utilized to grind rigid un plastisized PVC pipe, PVC profile,PVC board scraps into fine powder for recycling production waste in PVC plastic industry, it's necessary machine for UPVC plastic factory.

Features of Pulverizer/Mill

1. Saving energy consumption.

2. Environmental:this machine is used fully closed structure, no dust overflow. Separator in again a filter the outlet of dust catcher, effectively reduce the dust pollution.

3. Easy in maintenance:host door cover can open, easy for maintenance and replacement blades,tooth board.

4. The blade long life: the blade is high quality alloy steel, the blade can rotate 180 ° in quite a long time. The blade can continue to use (continuous use four times)after fastening.

5. Vibration feeding device: the machine absorb continuously by adapting electromagnet highspeed.

This equipment use to adapt feeding quantity avoiding the controls problem of valve feeding device.
