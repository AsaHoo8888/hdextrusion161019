Equipment overview

Vertical Color Mixer for ABS/PS/PP/PVC blending machine

Vertical Plastic PP LDPE HDPE ABS Pet Pellets Granules Drying Mixer

Plastic granule vertical agitating machine ,made of stainless steel, the machine adopts the state-of-the-art technique--spiral mixing, allows mixing storing in large quantity (up to 10 tones) . It is suitable for granular and powder forms of many materials, such as plastic and rubber raw material, animal food, wood sourced, building material, among other fields. The spiral at center elevates materials to top of the tank and then throw them outwards all around in tank to achieve blending purpose. The plastic granule vertical agitating machine overcomes the traditional shortcomings and disadvantages, allows most even and sufficient blending. It is the great aids for plastic extrusion, animal food mixing, building materials blending, and many others.

Features:

1. Material tank and propeller blades are made of stainless steel, eliminating rust corrosion and easy to wash;

2. The frame is of framework design, solid and durable;

3. Propeller is wholly processed after welding, with high concentricity and steady operation;

4. The standing operation allows little space;

5. Mixing in a sealed manner, uniform mixing and no leakage definitely;

6. Small horse-power, large mixing capacity and fast speed to realize a uniform mixing in 3~5 minutes;

7. Oil filler and material discharging device are designed at frame bottom, with durable bearings.
