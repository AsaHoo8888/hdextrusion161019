Before we configure
your line.

Is 400–800 mm the full range available?

No. The table describes the configuration listed on the source page. The wider HDPE family range and your project requirements are separate from that reference. Share the sizes you need for selection.

Are thickness measurement and dosing included?

The source describes these capabilities. Ask for a quotation that explicitly lists the dosing equipment, measurement system and control architecture included for your line.

Does total power indicate production energy use?

No. Source-listed total power does not establish running consumption, energy per kilogram or guaranteed output. Request the relevant utilities and performance details for your proposed configuration.
